# GRC risk assessment: UTS Hiking Society (fictional client)

Workshop exercise from "Think Like a Cyber Risk Consultant", run at CSECCon V (UTS Cyber Security Society) with Skylight Cyber Security on 12 September 2026.

In a team of four, I assessed how a fictional student club handles its members' personal data. My part was asking the interview questions, drafting the risks and writing the recommendations. Our evidence came from interviewing AI personas who played the club committee.

## What is in this repository

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `GRC_Risk_Assessment_UTS_Hiking_Society.pdf` | Full report: executive summary, scope and methodology, risk register, remediation plan, open questions |
| `evidence/` | Four screenshots from the interview with the treasurer persona |

## Result in short

- Six risks logged: two rated High, four Medium.
- The two High risks are access that exists today: shared role-account passwords that were never changed, and sheet access that is granted each year and never removed.
- Seven remediation actions, all free and sized for a four-person volunteer committee.

## Limits

The club, its committee and the incident context are fictional. Evidence is interviews only; no system was inspected. Risk ratings use a simple Low / Medium / High scale defined in the report. No framework is cited.
